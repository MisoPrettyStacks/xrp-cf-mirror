How it works

Every 5 minutes: fresh 5-minute candles → 21 features (volatility-scaled reversals, 4-hour regime, candle shape, clock-time) → LightGBM + logistic regression blend → isotonic calibration → shrinkage toward 50%.

Labels come from 6,427 settled 15-minute windows of free public market history — the same settlement feed the windows resolve on. No order-book data, no paid feeds.

A 15-minute crypto direction model built from free data should be expected to land within a point or two of 50%. This one is honest about that — and the live ledger below proves it.

Methodology in brief

  • Target: P(XRP closes above the window reference), resolved against the settlement feed.
  • Features (21): volatility-scaled returns at 6 horizons, prior-bar sign, RSI / Bollinger %B at 15-min and 4-hour, Parkinson volatility, volume z-score, candle body and wick ratios, quarter-hour / hour / weekday indicators.
  • Models: LightGBM (15 leaves, heavy regularization) + L2 logistic regression, blended 50/50, isotonic-calibrated, shrunk 50% toward 0.5.
  • Validation: walk-forward with 1-day embargo — no lookahead. A leak caught during development (fencepost error on candle timestamps) briefly showed AUC 0.79; fixed before it ever went live.
  • Honest ceiling: the literature puts free-data 15-min crypto direction at AUC 0.52–0.55. This model sits at the top of that range.
